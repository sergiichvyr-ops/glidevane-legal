Tilecharm – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Tilecharm ("the app") is published by Sergii Chvyr, Norway ("we"). Contact: prominentgamedev@gmail.com.

What we collect. We do not collect your name, email or account data. Your game progress (unlocked charms, Heat levels, Daily streak, the run in progress, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising. The app shows ads through Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data. Google uses this to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them if you consent.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases. Google Play processes in-app purchases. We receive confirmation of the purchase but no payment details.

Crash reports. From the first launch, the app sends crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix bugs. A report contains the error details, device model, Android version, app version, the round you were on and a random Firebase installation ID. It contains no name, email or advertising ID. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Crash reports are deleted after 90 days. You can turn them off with the Send crash reports switch in Settings.

Play statistics. Only if you tap "Sure" on the play stats card, the app sends anonymous gameplay events through Google Firebase Analytics, such as runs started and finished, rounds won and lost, charms chosen and Daily run results. These are linked to a random installation ID, not to you, and help us improve difficulty and features. You can turn this off at any time with the Share play stats switch in Settings. See Firebase privacy.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

Your rights (GDPR). You can withdraw ad consent in the app. You can turn off crash reports and play statistics in Settings. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can complain to Datatilsynet (Norway).

Changes. We will post updates here.
