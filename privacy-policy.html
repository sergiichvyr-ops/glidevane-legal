Glidevane – Privacy Policy (DRAFT – review before publishing)

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Glidevane ("the app") is published by Sergii Chvyr, Norway ("we"). Contact: prominentgamedev@gmail.com.

What we collect. We do not collect your name, email or account data. Your game progress (level, coins, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising. The app shows ads through Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data. Google uses this to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them if you consent.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested.
  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases. Google Play or the Apple App Store processes in-app purchases. We receive confirmation of the purchase but no payment details.

Analytics. If we enable analytics in a future version, we will update this policy first.

Note: the current version of the app sends no analytics or crash data off your device. A section on Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics is being prepared and will be added here before either is enabled in the app.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

Your rights (GDPR). You can withdraw ad consent in the app. For data held by Google, use Google's tools. You can complain to Datatilsynet (Norway).

Changes. We will post updates here.
