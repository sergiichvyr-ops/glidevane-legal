Comet Hop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Comet Hop ("the app", package com.prominentgame.comethop) is published by Prominent Game ("we"). Legal contact / controller: Sergii Chvyr, Norway. Contact: prominentgamedev@gmail.com.

What we collect. We do not collect your name, email or account data. Your game progress (unlocked skins, Star Dust balance, level progress, Daily results, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising. The app shows ads, including optional rewarded video ads and interstitial ads, through Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data. Google uses this to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them if you consent.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested.
  • You can change your ad consent at any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • See Google’s policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Crash reports. From the first launch, the app sends crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix bugs. A report contains the error details, device model, Android version, app version, the world and level you were on, and a random Firebase installation ID. It contains no name, email or advertising ID. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Crash reports are deleted after 90 days. You can turn them off with the Send crash reports switch in Settings.

Play statistics. Only if you opt in (for example by tapping “Sure” on the play stats card), the app sends anonymous gameplay events through Google Firebase Analytics, such as levels started, completed or failed, Daily puzzle results, and when a rewarded or interstitial ad is shown. These are linked to a random installation ID, not to you, and help us improve the game. Attached fields are limited to things like level ID, campaign/Daily flag, hops and stars. You can turn this off at any time with the Share play stats switch in Settings. See Firebase privacy.

Remote configuration. The app connects to Google Firebase Remote Config to fetch ad-pacing settings (for example whether interstitial ads are enabled, which campaign world they start in, how often they appear, and the minimum gap between them). This sends your Firebase installation ID and basic device info (model, OS version, app version). It does not include your name, email or advertising ID. See Firebase privacy.

Permissions. The app requests Internet access, network state, wake lock (kept briefly by Firebase Analytics while events are sent), and the advertising ID permission used by AdMob. It does not request notification permission.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

Your rights (GDPR). You can withdraw ad consent in the app. You can turn off crash reports and play statistics in Settings. For data held by Google, use Google’s tools. You can complain to Datatilsynet (Norway).

Changes. We will post updates here.
