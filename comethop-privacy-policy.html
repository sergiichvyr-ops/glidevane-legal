Comethop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Comethop ("the app") is published by Sergii Chvyr, Norway ("we"). Contact: prominentgamedev@gmail.com.

What we collect. We do not collect your name, email or account data. Your game progress (unlocked skins, Star Dust balance, settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising. The app shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob and Meta Audience Network (via AdMob mediation).

  • AdMob and Meta may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location and ad interaction data. These partners use this to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them if you consent.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested.
  • Until the app is publicly launched, test ads are shown.
  • You can change your ad consent at any time in Settings > Privacy options.
  • See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • See Meta's policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

Purchases. Google Play processes in-app purchases (such as the remove-ads option). We receive confirmation of the purchase but no payment details.

Crash reports. From the first launch, the app sends crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix bugs. A report contains the error details, device model, Android version, app version, the game state you were in, and a random Firebase installation ID. It contains no name, email or advertising ID. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Crash reports are deleted after 90 days. You can turn them off with the Send crash reports switch in Settings.

Play statistics. Only if you opt in, the app sends anonymous gameplay events through Google Firebase Analytics, such as levels completed, Star Dust earned and skins unlocked. These are linked to a random installation ID, not to you, and help us improve the game. You can turn this off at any time with the Share play stats switch in Settings. See Firebase privacy.

Remote configuration. The app connects to Google Firebase Remote Config to fetch game settings and feature flags (such as level difficulty or event availability). This sends your Firebase installation ID and basic device info (model, OS version, app version). No personal data is sent. See Firebase privacy.

Notifications. Only if you say Yes to the daily reminder, the app schedules one local reminder a day on your device. Reminders are created on the device; no notification data is sent to us or to anyone else. On Android 13 and later the system also asks for notification permission. You can turn reminders off in Settings or in Android's notification settings.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

Your rights (GDPR). You can withdraw ad consent in the app. You can turn off crash reports and play statistics in Settings. For data held by Google or Meta, use their tools. You can complain to Datatilsynet (Norway).

Changes. We will post updates here.
